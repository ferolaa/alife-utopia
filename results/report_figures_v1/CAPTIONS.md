**phase1_paired_independence**

Each line joins one independent training-seed pair. Each point averages 40 assigned-pup trials (20 fixed worlds × two energies); diamonds are ten-seed means. These are actual independence outcomes, not directional pup seeking. Parent follow-up ends at pup death or tick 60.

**phase2_independence**

Each cell has 200 trials (ten training seeds × 20 fixed worlds). Error bars: unadjusted 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over ten training seeds, conditional on the world panel. Background bodies are stationary, resource-neutral evaluation proxies; OFF/ON refers to the programmed care-disruption flag. All energy-20 parents survive.

**phase2_care_mechanism**

Both energies pooled; 400 trials per bar. Denominator is observed dependent-pup care ticks, ending at death or maturation (maximum 59 per trial). Red is baseline attendance invalidated by active crowding disruption. Grey includes out-of-range or dead parents. This denominator varies by survival and is not a common 60-tick mortality risk.

**supplement_paired_penalties**

Lines join the two trained policies within each seed. Units are percentage points, not relative percentages. Survival policies reach zero independence in high/ON; their lower baseline bounds the absolute penalty. This plot cannot establish intrinsically greater offspring-policy susceptibility.
