The descriptive average interaction is −16.67 percentage points. Only two training pairs are independent replicates; no p-values or confirmatory intervals are reported.

Attendance percentages use observed care ticks and are affected by earlier pup death; the matched CSV also records raw attended ticks. All failed assigned pups die of neglect. No success occurs after parental death because every parent survives.

Recommendation: a full, separately approved study could be informative as a modest frozen-policy sensitivity/causal-ablation result. The pilot neither establishes lasting neural damage nor a reliable difference between training objectives. Persistence is programmed, not emergent. A null or opposite full result remains acceptable. No dose escalation, seed selection, recovery or network change is justified.
