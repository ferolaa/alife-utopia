# Supplied-care v3.0: proposed pilot, not executed

This protocol replaces the learning task, not the historical null result. Scientific execution requires independent review and separate authorization. The review CLI has no pilot launch option. No PPO, crowding campaign, transfer training, final-test evaluation or hyperparameter search is part of this implementation.

## Task and estimand

Each world starts with one mature parent (age 120) and one genuine newborn dependent pup, with an owned nest and reciprocal parent/dependent links. The shared neural policy supplies all adult actions, including the pup's actions from its first adult tick. The six original actions remain available; reproduction attempts fail because this task's reproduction threshold is infinite. There is no action mask, demonstrated action, proximity reward, attendance reward, survival override or scripted assistance to neural policies.

All movement, local senses, food consumption/replenishment, energy expenditure, age, neglect recovery/death, maturation and nest release use the original Agent/World methods. The full-colony configuration and reproduction mechanics are unchanged. Pre-action observations retain the original collector ordering: they precede Agent.act's cost/age/maturation processing.

The factorial initial-state distribution has 18 conditions: nearest initial food distance 4/6/8 (Chebyshev squares), parent energy 6/12/20, and initial parent-to-pup distance 0/4 toward the food patch. Independently seeded geometry varies 24/32-square worlds, walls/torus, four orientations, nest translation, and feeder spread 0/1. One feeder contains 1/3 food items, with original unlimited-food replenishment. The nest lies outside the food patch. Body costs and food energy are inherited from UNIVERSE_25, including the original contact cost. There are no additional creatures or crowding manipulations.

The finite horizon is **100 ticks**: 60 to natural independence plus 40 of subsequent follow-up. Independence and pup death do not terminate the world. Both-dead extinction has an explicit absorbing remainder. A living pup counts as independent only after surviving its first adult action at zero-based tick 59; a pup dying during that action earns no bonus. Follow-up and survival at tick 100 remain separate from independence. A pup can legitimately become independent after parent death. This is reported, not removed or relabeled as parental competence.

This task estimates caregiving competence conditional on receiving a pup. It does not estimate finding a mate/nest, deciding to reproduce, raising repeated litters or sustaining a full colony. Transfer to the unmodified full colony is a subsequent study requiring a separately frozen protocol. Existing full-colony collectors/evaluation remain available, but no transfer evaluation or training is run here.

## Reward and gradient accounting

Both objectives use **colony** credit and gamma **0.99**. Survival-only pays .01 to each adult surviving its actual action; dependent pups receive no adult survival payment. The offspring objective adds 1.0 to the biological parent once per pup surviving its first adult action, including posthumous payment. Both rewards use the original coefficients. Evaluation records use a common explicitly labeled offspring reward basis, irrespective of training objective; the primary evaluation endpoint is physiological independence, not objective-dependent return.

The estimand is J = E[sum_t gamma^t R_colony(t)]. For every sampled adult action at t, its policy score is weighted by gamma^t times G_colony(t), where G includes all subsequent colony events on the real world clock. This includes rewards after that actor dies. Four independently seeded complete worlds are collected at unchanged actor weights; actor score sums are divided by **four worlds**, never a policy-dependent number of actions. Exactly one actor update follows each batch, without reuse, return normalization, clipping or entropy bonus. No truncated world enters a Monte Carlo update; finite horizon/extinction has no bootstrap value.

The learned baseline is the existing separate 12→64→64→1 critic. It predicts per-action colony return from the local observation. Its prediction is detached from the actor advantage and obtained before fitting that batch. A separate Adam optimizer then minimizes return MSE. The observation omits the full colony/time state, so a poor critic remains possible. Report calibration, advantages, actor gradient norms, entropy and actual behavior; low predictive explained variance alone does not establish failure to reduce policy-gradient variance.

At independence the absolute event discount is .99^59 = .5527, versus approximately .08 at the old pilot's median paid-bonus time. This improves reward exposure but changes the task distribution; comparisons to the old pilot cannot isolate an algorithmic improvement. No guarantee of successful learning follows.

## Splits and evaluation

- Training world/action seed: 2,100,000 + 10,000 × replicate seed + episode index. At most 10,000 episodes per replicate; training block is [2,100,000, 2,200,000). Conditions cycle in the declared 18-condition order, identical across algorithms/objectives/replicates. Local Python/Torch RNGs isolate training from evaluation.
- Validation: seeds 3,000,300 through 3,000,303, crossed with all 18 conditions = **72 trials**, shared across all policies. These are four geometry-seed clusters, not 72 independent experimental replicates. Repeated checkpoints reuse the same panel for paired descriptive learning curves.
- Sealed final panel design: 20 fresh seeds 4,000,300 through 4,000,319 × 18 conditions = 360 trials. These seeds are declared only: no worlds have been instantiated, inspected or selected. Existing reserved final seeds 4,000,000 through 4,000,019 and all original historical final sets remain sealed. The task API rejects the entire [4,000,000, 5,000,000) namespace. Opening any final panel requires separate authorization/code review after the selection protocol is frozen.

Before training, evaluate untrained neural seeds 0/1, the unchanged hand-designed heuristic, uniform policy, fixed care, abandonment, and an observation-only care/foraging control. The control's fixed decisions never enter neural action selection. For every later trained policy, use identical initial worlds and action seeds, frozen actor weights, no critic/optimizer update, restored mode, and checked state hashes. Common random numbers match initial states; action-dependent food consumption can legitimately cause later environmental trajectories to diverge.

Primary endpoint: independent pups / supplied pups (72). Also report parent-alive independence / supplied pups; posthumous independence count and / independent pups; pup survival at tick 100; parent survival at tick 100 / parents supplied; attended dependent ticks / observed dependent ticks; successful parent/pup feeding events; parent feeds / parent at-risk ticks; parent/pup adult decisions; causes/times of death. Report all condition strata, especially energy 6 and food distance 8. Never pool these denominators with natural eligible births in full-colony experiments. Posthumous outcomes remain real offspring success, with a distinct biological interpretation.

The initial development check used energies 10/20 and revealed 100% fixed-care independence. Before any scientific training, the panel was revised to include energy 6: without eating a parent dies by tick 24 at the latest, too early for neglect tolerance 25 to bridge to independence at tick 60. Final energies are 6/12/20. This is documented assay development, not a hidden training result or an untouched held-out test. All preliminary review outputs are retained outside the repository. The final heuristic is not perfect in the resulting panel (48/72).

## Eight cells and fairness

2 algorithms (V2 REINFORCE, V2 REINFORCE + learned baseline) × 2 objectives (survival, survival + offspring) × replicate seeds 0/1. Actor: original **12→12→6**, 234 parameters. Adam actor lr .001; critic lr .001; gamma .99; colony scope; no normalization, entropy, clipping, PPO or tuning. Tuning allowance is zero in every cell.

Within each replicate all four cells share initial actor weights, condition order and world/action seed prefixes. The critic's 5,057 parameters add compute but do not change actor capacity or initialization. All three actor architectures have compatibility tests/smokes; no architecture campaign is proposed here. In future architecture comparisons, match tasks, seed prefixes, interactions, evaluation and tuning, report parameter counts and wall time, and distinguish capacity from compute efficiency. Equal parameter counts cannot be claimed.

Target **20,000 adult decisions per cell**, counting parent and newly independent pup actions, including fatal actions. Collect **four complete worlds per optimizer update**. A world has at most 100 parent + 41 pup decisions = 141; a batch at most 564. Stop at the **first complete batch** reaching the target, regardless of success, with no extra batch to improve the result. Maximum excess and between-cell spread: **563 decisions (2.815%)**. Publish realized budgets and update/episode counts. Policy-dependent survival changes episodes/updates per interaction budget; this is a disclosed comparison limitation. No arbitrary fixed number of updates is imposed to erase it.

Validate at initialization and the first completed batch crossing 5k, 10k, 15k, 20k; record actual counts. Validation interactions are excluded from training budgets. Save initial and **every** subsequent batch-boundary checkpoint, hashes, source/runtime metadata, full per-world transitions/reward events/metrics and append-only JSONL. This prevents the old pilot's missing-preupdate-policy diagnosis limitation. Exclusive paths prevent overwrites; safe restoration checks checksum, task, runtime and source hashes. Old checkpoints remain bound to their archived source; current-source restoration rejecting them is intentional.

## Budget informativeness, gates and interpretation

20k decisions guarantee at least 36 updates (144 supplied pups) and typically provide more, because many episodes contain fewer than 141 decisions. The bounded original-MLP smoke's first four worlds produced 307 decisions/update; extrapolation is approximately 65 updates/260 supplied pups, **not** a measured learning trajectory or a promised sample size. The 72-trial initial validation has visible headroom and behavior-sensitive strata. These facts make 20k informative about early feasibility, not convergence or superiority. No budget extension is automatic.

Correctness gates before execution: independent review of the diff and protocol; all tests pass; preservation inventories match; source/runtime provenance frozen; matched initialization and seeds; no final access; no births/cap artifacts; no truncated Monte Carlo batches; natural maturation and correct single bonus; frozen validation; finite losses/gradients/weights; checkpoint replay and exclusive-write checks. Halt and preserve all evidence on a correctness violation or exhausted episode/batch guard. One cell failing correctness stops campaign interpretation until reviewed. Poor scientific performance is **not** a correctness failure.

Report per-seed, per-cell initial-to-final changes and all five learning-curve points; paired validation gains/losses; attendance, feeding, living-parent independence and survival tradeoffs; realized reward exposure, entropy and gradient/value diagnostics. Interpret improvement, deterioration, heterogeneity or no measured improvement at the fixed budget. Do not demand an arbitrary success threshold in every seed, discard a weak seed, select the best checkpoint retrospectively, or claim significance from two seeds. The final budget checkpoint is the descriptive endpoint. A positive caregiving result alone does not establish full-colony generalization.

## Compute and execution status

CPU, two Torch threads, pinned existing environment; no added dependencies. Successful original-MLP technical batches took .142 s (REINFORCE) / .150 s (baseline), including checkpoint/restore overhead, for 307 decisions each. Final frozen 72-trial neural panels took 1.8–2.2 s while tests also ran. Pure projections: approximately 1.5 minutes of training interaction/update work and 2 minutes for 40 validation panels, plus checkpoint/source/JSON I/O. Reserve **4–10 minutes CPU wall time and roughly 1–4 GB** for the eight-cell pilot; this is an estimate, not a run or guaranteed storage bound. Preserve all batch checkpoints; do not thin them to save space without review.

Training is at most 164,504 decisions including whole-batch excess. Validation is at most 406,080 decisions (8×5×72×141), separately accounted. There is no scientific pilot launch flag in `experiments/care_feasibility_v2.py`: it only evaluates controls and, with `--smoke`, performs six one-batch compatibility checks. The single-cell `CareExperiment.train_to_budget` API implements the fixed stop/archive rule and is tested with a synthetic counter, not executed at 20k.

After independent review, an explicitly authorized launcher can call that API for the eight declared cells, verify aggregate budget spread and preflight preservation, and archive the resulting campaign. No such campaign has been launched here.
